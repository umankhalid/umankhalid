## About Me

I am a Research Professor at **Kyung Hee University, Korea**, working on integrated quantum information technologies spanning **quantum communication, sensing, secure networking, computing, and artificial intelligence**.

My research follows three complementary directions:

- **Classical-for-Quantum:** AI/ML methods for quantum-system characterization, sensing, and network operation.
- **Quantum-for-Classical:** quantum resources and hybrid quantum-classical learning for emerging 6G, optimization, sensing, and communication applications.
- **Hybrid Quantum-Classical Systems:** practical architectures that jointly exploit quantum and classical processing under realistic resource constraints.

More recently, my work on **Rydberg atomic receivers** has motivated a growing interest in experimental quantum engineering and deployable quantum technologies.

## Research Themes

### Quantum Resource Characterization, Sensing, and Metrology
My work investigates how quantum resources can be identified, quantified, and exploited for sensing and information processing. Topics include measurement-based quantum correlations, multipartite entanglement, quantum state reconstruction, machine-learning-assisted quantum-property detection, secure metrology, variational sensing, distributed sensing, and quantum clock synchronization.

### Quantum Networking and Anonymous Communication
I develop quantum networking protocols emphasizing **privacy, anonymity, security, and resource efficiency**, including anonymous collision detection, anonymous private information retrieval, full-duplex quantum communication, counterfactual communication, and NISQ-era network engineering.

### Quantum Semantic Communication and AI-Native Networks
My research explores quantum-assisted semantic communication architectures integrating **task-oriented information processing, AI, quantum communication, and 6G systems**, including privacy-preserving semantic broadcast, industrial CPS networks, and generative-AI-enabled communication testbeds.

### Quantum-Enhanced AI for 6G
I work on hybrid quantum-classical learning architectures that combine parameterized quantum circuits with modern AI models for **computer vision, ISAC, remote sensing, multimodal learning, and intelligent wireless systems**.

### Quantum Optimization
My research also includes variational quantum optimization, QAOA, VQE, quantum recurrent models, and hybrid learning for transportation, scheduling, sensing infrastructure, and combinatorial optimization.
