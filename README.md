## About

I work on **integrated quantum information technologies** spanning:

- **Quantum communication**
- **Quantum sensing and metrology**
- **Quantum computing and machine learning**
- **Secure quantum networking**
- **Hybrid quantum-classical AI for future 6G systems**

My research connects three directions:

- **Classical-for-Quantum** → AI/ML for quantum-system characterization and operation  
- **Quantum-for-Classical** → quantum-enhanced learning, optimization, and communications  
- **Hybrid Systems** → practical architectures combining quantum and classical intelligence
